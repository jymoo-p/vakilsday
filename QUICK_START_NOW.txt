STEP 1: Get Google Credentials (2 min)
---------------------------------------
Open: https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials

Click your OAuth 2.0 Client → Copy:
- Client ID
- Client secret


STEP 2: Add Localhost Redirect (1 min)
---------------------------------------
In same Google Console page:
- "Authorized redirect URIs" → + ADD URI
- Enter: http://localhost:3000/api/auth/callback/google
- Click SAVE


STEP 3: Update .env.local (1 min)
----------------------------------
Open: .env.local

Replace these two lines with your actual values:
GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID="PASTE_YOUR_CLIENT_ID_HERE"
GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET="PASTE_YOUR_CLIENT_SECRET_HERE"

Save the file.


STEP 4: Start the App (1 min)
------------------------------
Run this command:

    ./START_LOCAL.sh

OR manually:

    npx prisma generate
    npm run dev


STEP 5: Test Sign In
--------------------
1. Open: http://localhost:3000
2. Click "Sign In"
3. Click "Sign In with Google"
4. Sign in with your account
